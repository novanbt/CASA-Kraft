Frequently Addressed Questions

How does Casa Kraft Interiors ensure quality on-site? expand_more

Every site is assigned a dedicated senior project architect and full-time site engineer. We do not sublet work to unsupervised sub-contractors. Our carpenters, stonemasons, and polishers belong to our permanent atelier guild.

Can we visit live on-going villa projects before finalizing? expand_more

Yes, with client consent. We frequently arrange private walkthroughs of our active sites so prospective patrons can inspect our joinery tolerances, electrical conduit discipline, and stone installation firsthand.

What warranty coverage is included in turnkey execution? expand_more

We provide a written 10-Year Assured Warranty on all core substrates (calibrated BWP marine ply) and hardware mechanisms (Blum, Hafele, Hettich), plus a 1-year complimentary preventative maintenance pass covering touch-ups and alignment checks.

Do you handle municipal approvals and gated community civil permits? expand_more

Yes. Our studio coordinates drawing submissions and architectural compliance dossiers directly with gated community committees, residential societies, and development managers to ensure frictionless approvals.
