## ✨ Features & Functionality

1. **Flagship Home (`index.html`)**
   - High-resolution hero with verified location badge and quick metric counters (110+ Homes, 09 Yrs Practice, 100% Turnkey).
   - Philosophy & 3 Atelier Pillars (Material Authenticity, Contextual Geometry, Calm Living).
   - Asymmetric Bento Grid highlighting signature projects.
   - 4-Stage Execution Timeline (Understand, Design, Plan, Deliver).
   - Homeowner reflections & client care details.

2. **Services & Scope Calculator (`services.html`)**
   - 4 Core Service Modules: Residential Interiors, Modular Kitchens & Wardrobes, Space Renovations, Commercial Sanctuaries.
   - **Interactive Turnkey Scope & Budget Calculator**:
     - Live Project Nature rate selector (Villa, Apartment, Commercial).
     - Live Carpet Area slider (1,000 sq.ft to 10,000+ sq.ft).
     - Curated finish upgrade toggles (Blum hardware, Italian Travertine, Smart DALI lighting, Civil demolition).
     - Instant calculation of estimated budget in ₹ Lakhs and completion schedule in business days.
     - One-click WhatsApp link pre-filled with the calculated estimate.
   - 5-Stage Casa Kraft Studio Protocol.
   - Frequently Addressed Questions accordion.

3. **Selected Works & Monograph Drawer (`projects.html`)**
   - Live category filter bar (All Works, Residential, Kitchens & Wardrobes, Living Spaces, Commercial).
   - **Slide-Over Project Monograph Drawer**:
     - Opens on card click with high-resolution photography.
     - Architectural brief narrative & design objectives.
     - Tactile Materiality Palette swatch cards (Roman Travertine, Burmese Teak, Aged Brass, Belgian Linen).
     - Project metrics (Square footage, Execution duration, Location, Scope).
     - One-click inquiry trigger.

4. **Studio Story & Material Provenance (`about.html`)**
   - Atelier founding philosophy: quiet luxury, daylight pacing, and Deccan climate responsiveness.
   - Provenance gallery of authentic unglazed stone, quarter-sawn teak, and forged brass.

5. **Private Inquiries & Studio Booking (`contact.html`)**
   - Architectural Dossier submission form with live validation and toast notifications.
   - Studio experience coordinates, operating hours, and private consultation.
   - Direct WhatsApp Concierge integration.
